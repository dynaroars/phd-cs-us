# Faculty Applications

[Guide home](README.md)

A guide to the computer science faculty job market in the United States. Start by comparing roles and building a shortlist, then prepare the application package, practice the interview, and evaluate the offer.

[Finding positions and choosing a track](#finding-positions-and-choosing-a-track) · [Planning the search](#planning-the-search) · [Application materials and examples](#application-materials-and-examples) · [Interviews, job talks, and chalk talks](#interviews-job-talks-and-chalk-talks) · [Offers and negotiation](#offers-and-negotiation)

## Finding positions and choosing a track

- [CRA Career Center](https://careercenter.cra.org/) — computing research job listings.
- [ACM Career and Job Center](https://jobs.acm.org/) — computing positions across academia and industry.
- [AcademicJobsOnline computer science listings](https://academicjobsonline.org/ajo/cs/All%20areas) — academic openings and application portals.
- [CS Jobs at Primarily Undergraduate Institutions](https://cs-pui.github.io/) — computer science positions and resources for applicants interested in undergraduate-focused departments.
- [CRA teaching faculty resources](https://cra.org/teaching-faculty/) — hiring and evaluation practices for teaching-focused computing roles.
- [CMU Computer Science faculty hiring](https://www.csd.cmu.edu/employment/faculty-hiring) and [UW–Madison Computer Sciences recruitment](https://www.cs.wisc.edu/people/faculty-staff-recruitment/) — examples of departmental job pages and application instructions.
- [CRA Taulbee Survey](https://cra.org/resources/taulbee-survey/) — data on computing doctoral enrollment, degree production, and employment.
- [A Periodic Table of Faculty Job Titles at US Universities](<https://shomir.net/faculty_periodic_table.html>) — Shomir Wilson explains U.S. faculty job titles and the appointment types applicants may encounter.
- [Teaching Track Faculty](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/education/teaching-faculty/) — Carnegie Mellon School of Computer Science’s description of teaching-track ranks, appointment terms, and duties; CMU-specific, useful for comparing a teaching-track offer with a tenure-track role.
- [Fall Jobs Post 2026](https://blog.computationalcomplexity.org/2026/11/fall-jobs-post-2026.html) — Lance Fortnow’s annual outlook on the faculty job market, focused on theoretical computer science, with application advice and links to theory, CRA, and ACM job boards (November 2026).
