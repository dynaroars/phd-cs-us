# Prospective PhD Students

[Guide home](README.md)

Explore research, decide whether a PhD fits your goals, and prepare an application to U.S. computer science programs. Use [PhD Demystify](README.md#phd-demystify) for a walkthrough of admissions and [CSPicks](https://cspicks.roars.dev) to build a program and faculty shortlist.
