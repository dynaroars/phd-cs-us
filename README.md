# PhD Demystify

**PhD Demystify** is an insider’s guide to computer science PhD admissions in the United States. It explains how admissions work and helps prospective students, especially applicants from abroad or from less well-known institutions, prepare to apply. The companion tool, **CSPicks**, helps students explore faculty, programs, and research areas.

- Read the [free PDF](https://roars.dev/phd-cs-us/demystify.pdf) or [view the PDF on GitHub](https://github.com/dynaroars/phd-cs-us/blob/main/demystify.pdf).
- Get the [Kindle edition](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0F41HP6Y2).
- Explore computer science programs and faculty with [CSPicks](https://cspicks.roars.dev).
