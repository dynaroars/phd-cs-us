# Faculty Resources

[Guide home](README.md)

Resources for starting a faculty position, building a research group, advising and teaching, securing funding, and navigating service and promotion. Use the official policies and deadlines at your institution and funding agency when acting on a requirement.

## Starting a faculty position

- [Career Mentoring Workshop](https://cra.org/career-mentoring-workshop/) — CRA’s workshop for future and early-career computing faculty.
- [Making the Right Moves](https://www.hhmi.org/sites/default/files/2023-10/making-the-right-moves-second-edition.pdf) — HHMI and Burroughs Wellcome Fund’s handbook on scientific management for postdocs and new faculty.
- [Faculty mentoring](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/faculty-resources/mentoring/) — Cornell Engineering’s example of a mentoring program for new faculty.
- [Faculty development programs](https://www.ncfdd.org/Programs/) — NCFDD workshops and programs on writing, planning, and career development.

## Building a lab and mentoring

- [ROARS Lab Manual](lab-manual.pdf) and [ROARS Lab Advising Guide](advising.pdf) — ThanhVu Nguyen’s examples of lab expectations and advising practices.
- [Managing a Research Group](https://research.columbia.edu/managing-research-group) — Columbia’s guidance on building and managing a group.
- [A guide to setting up and managing a lab at a research-intensive institution](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12919-021-00214-7) — practical advice on hiring, budgeting, and leadership.
- [An open lab manual](https://www.compgeolab.org/manual/) — Computer-Oriented Geoscience Lab’s example of documenting group practices.
- [How to Mentor Graduate Students](https://rackham.umich.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/faculty-mentoring-handbook-2024.pdf) — Michigan Rackham’s faculty mentoring handbook.
- [Faculty Guide to Advising Research Degree Students](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/home/resources/faculty-guide-to-advising-research-degree-students/) — Cornell’s advice on expectations, feedback, and student progress.
- [Entering Mentoring](https://cimer.wcer.wisc.edu/mentor-mentee-training/entering-mentoring-training/) — CIMER’s research mentor training approach.
- [Shared expectations and mentoring templates](https://rackham.umich.edu/faculty-and-staff/resources-for-directors/mentoring/) — Michigan Rackham materials for advisor and student conversations.
- [Supervising doctoral students in France, part 1](https://journals.openedition.org/echogeo/22889) and [part 2](https://journals.openedition.org/echogeo/23589) — perspectives on doctoral supervision and working conditions, in French.

## Funding and proposal development

- [NSF proposal and award process](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/overview) and [submitting a proposal](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/submitting-proposal) — official guidance from opportunity selection through submission.
- [NSF Proposal and Award Policies and Procedures Guide](https://www.nsf.gov/policies/pappg) — check the version and supplemental notices applicable to your submission.
- [NSF Computer and Information Science and Engineering](https://www.nsf.gov/cise) and [NSF Award Search](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/) — locate computing opportunities and read abstracts of funded projects.
- [Faculty Early Career Development Program (CAREER)](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/career-faculty-early-career-development-program) — NSF’s program page and solicitation for integrated plans in research and education.
- [NSF CISE CAREER proposal writing workshop](https://www.nsf.gov/events/2023-nsf-cise-career-proposal-writing-workshop) — workshop materials and context for computing faculty.
- [Broader impacts](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/learn/broader-impacts) — NSF’s guidance on connecting research to societal outcomes.
- [Manage your NSF award](https://www.nsf.gov/awards) — post-award reporting, points of contact, and requests for award changes.
- [U.S. Department of Energy Early Career Research Program](https://science.osti.gov/early-career) and [DARPA Young Faculty Award](https://www.darpa.mil/work-with-us/communities/academia/young-faculty-award) — additional federal programs to check for fit and eligibility.
- [Catalyzing Interdisciplinary Computing Research](https://cra.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Catalyzing-Interdisciplinary-Computing-Research-Best-Practices-for-Researchers_August-2024_FINAL.pdf) — CRA on building collaborations across disciplines.
- [Advice on writing an NSF CAREER proposal—or any proposal](https://cragl.cs.gmu.edu/career.html) — Yotam Gingold’s Creativity and Graphics Lab at George Mason University, on writing for a broad-field panel and planning evaluation, risks, ethics, and data management; check the current CAREER solicitation for requirements.

## Research integrity and lab operations

- [Introduction to the Responsible Conduct of Research](https://ori.hhs.gov/education/products/RCRintro/) — Office of Research Integrity materials on collaboration, data management, and authorship.
- [Human subject regulations decision charts](https://www.hhs.gov/ohrp/regulations-and-policy/decision-charts-2018/index.html) — U.S. Office for Human Research Protections materials to discuss with your institution’s IRB.
- [NSF engineering data management and sharing guidance](https://www.nsf.gov/eng/data-management-sharing-plans) — an example of sponsor- and directorate-specific planning requirements.
- [ACM Policy on Authorship](https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/new-acm-policy-on-authorship) and [CRediT contributor roles](https://credit.niso.org/) — references for discussing authorship and documenting contributions.
- [ACM artifact review and badging](https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/artifact-review-and-badging-current) — artifact evaluation terminology and expectations.

## Teaching and curriculum

- [Teach Your Course](https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/designteach/teach/index.html) — Carnegie Mellon’s Eberly Center guidance on teaching and managing a course.
- [Active learning strategies](https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/resources/instructionalstrategies/activelearningstrategies/index.html) and [grading and feedback](https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/designteach/inclusiveexcellence/gradingandfeedback/index.html) — Eberly Center resources on classroom activities, grading, and feedback.
- [CS2023 Computer Science Curricula](https://csed.acm.org/) — ACM, IEEE Computer Society, and AAAI guidelines for undergraduate computing curricula.
- [SIGCSE](https://sigcse.org/) — ACM’s computing education community, with conferences and professional resources.
- [UDL Tips for Assessment](https://www.cast.org/resources/tips-articles/udl-tips-assessment/) — CAST’s guidance on accessible assessment design.
- [OpenDSA](https://github.com/OpenDSA/OpenDSA) — open instructional modules for data structures and algorithms.
- [Accessible course content](https://teachingsupport.umn.edu/accessible-and-inclusive-teaching/accessible-course-content) — University of Minnesota guidance on making course materials accessible.

## Service, hiring, and professional conduct

- [Conference planning roles](https://www.sigcomm.sigcomm.hosting2.acm.org/conference-planning/sigcomm-planning/conference-roles) — ACM SIGCOMM’s breakdown of organizer responsibilities.
- [Organizing a SIGCHI-sponsored conference](https://sigchi.org/resources/guides-for-organizers/organising-a-sigchi-sponsored-or-co-sponsored-conference/) — ACM SIGCHI’s workflow for conference organizers.
- [ACM SIGPLAN conference submission review policy](https://www.sigplan.org/Resources/Policies/Review/) — an example of committee responsibilities, confidentiality, and conflict handling.
- [ACM Code of Ethics](https://www.acm.org/code-of-ethics) and [Policy Against Harassment at ACM Activities](https://www.acm.org/about-acm/policy-against-harassment) — professional conduct and reporting guidance.
- [Handbook of Best Practices for Faculty Searches](https://ap.washington.edu/faculty-inclusive-excellence/faculty-search/handbook-of-best-practices-for-faculty-searches/) — University of Washington’s guide for search committees.
- [Best Practices for Hiring Teaching Faculty in Research Computing Departments](https://cra.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Best-Practices-for-Hiring-Teaching-Faculty-in-Research-Computing-Departments-White-Paper-March-2024.pdf) — CRA’s recommendations on recruitment and evaluation.

## Annual review, tenure, and promotion

- [A Short Guide to the Tenure Process](https://facultydevelopment.cornell.edu/faculty-development/tenure-and-promotion/a-short-guide-to-the-tenure-process/) and [Cornell’s faculty handbook on tenure-track promotions](https://deanoffaculty.cornell.edu/policies-procedures/faculty-handbook/4-tenure-track-promotions-2/) — institutional examples for understanding review and dossier expectations.
- [Evaluating Scholarship in Hiring, Tenure, and Promotion](https://cra.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/BP_Memo.pdf) — CRA’s memo on evaluating the quality and impact of computing scholarship.
- [Promotion and Tenure of Interdisciplinary Faculty](https://archive.cra.org/reports/promotion_tenure.html) — CRA’s guidance on recognizing work that crosses departmental boundaries.
